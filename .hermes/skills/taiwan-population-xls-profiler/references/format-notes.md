# Source format notes
- Sheets normally represent ROC year 114 months 01-12.
- Titles, merged headers, age-band totals, and single-age columns coexist.
- A region name may appear on the male row after its combined-sex total row.
- Province rows are aggregates; combining them with constituent counties double-counts population.
- Detect columns by meaning, not position. December may differ from prior months.
